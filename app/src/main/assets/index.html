How to ring out a mic

  1. Set up the mic and speakers where they will be used. Put the phone near the mic position.
  2. Tap Start mic. Slowly raise the mixer gain until a tone starts to ring.
  3. The ringing frequency shows up in red. Tap Notch (narrow cut) or Cut band −3 (graphic EQ).
  4. Raise the gain a little more and repeat. Stop after 4–6 notches, then back the gain off 3 dB for safety.

Monitor out sends the processed mic to the phone's output (speaker, headphones or a cable to your amp). Android adds some delay, so the best use is often: find the frequencies here, then cut them on your mixer's EQ. Use Auto-notch only while ringing out, not during music, because sustained notes can look like feedback.
